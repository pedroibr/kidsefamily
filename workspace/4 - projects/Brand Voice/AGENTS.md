# Project: Brand Voice

## Purpose

Define a practical, reusable voice for Clínica Kids e Family across website copy, social content, banners, campaigns, and patient communication.

## Inheritance

- Inherit all root, workspace, and projects rules automatically.
- The clinic's current strategy and business context remain the sources for facts, positioning, audience, and clinical scope.

## Rules

- Use [Brand Voice](Brand%20Voice.md) as the project's working guide. Treat recommendations as hypotheses until the clinic team validates them.
- Keep deck content, interpretation, and clinic-specific adaptations distinct. Never present a proposed combination of Archetype Pack and Copywriting Tactics as an official deck recipe.
- Do not turn archetypes into fixed labels for patients or staff; use them as flexible lenses for brand expression and storytelling.
- Use Portuguese from Portugal for clinic-facing examples, following Business Info, unless the user requests another locale.
- Do not invent clinical claims, causes, outcomes, testimonials, patient stories, or clinic processes. Have clinical claims and patient-facing examples reviewed before publication.
- Preserve the existing guardrails against fear, guilt, artificial urgency, overclaiming, and implying that every health issue begins in the mouth.

## Routing

- Keep this project's voice guide and working examples in this folder.
- Keep durable positioning and brand decisions in the [Strategy Workspace](../../1%20-%20strategy/INDEX.md).
- Keep canonical business, audience, and clinical context in the [Context Workspace](../../2%20-%20context/INDEX.md).
- Route production of a specific campaign or asset through the [Projects Workspace](../INDEX.md) or [Operations Workspace](../../5%20-%20operation/INDEX.md).
