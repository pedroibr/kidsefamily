# Campaign: Odontopediatria + Anúncios Diretos por Interesse

## Purpose

This folder contains the central brief and operational assets for this campaign.

## Inheritance

- Inherit the rules of the root workspace, workspace layer, operation area, and campaigns area.
- Read `campaign.md` before creating, changing, or evaluating any campaign asset.
- Read the selected asset's `briefing.md` before producing its content.

## Rules

- Treat `campaign.md` as the source of truth for scope, decisions, approvals, and asset status.
- Do not advance an asset past a required approval gate; record explicit approvals in the relevant document.
- Keep campaign-wide decisions in `campaign.md` and asset-specific decisions in that asset's folder.
- Do not invent clinical facts, proof, metrics, budget, or approval.
- Public-facing healthcare advertising requires clinical and regulatory review before activation.
- Do not publish, send, schedule, buy media, or change external systems from this folder.
- Do not store credentials, tokens, or sensitive personal data.

## Routing

- Campaign scope, status, and asset map: `campaign.md`.
- Ad briefs, source copy, and platform adaptations: `ads/`.
- Shared campaign navigation and history: `INDEX.md`, `MEMORY.md`, and `LOG.md`.
