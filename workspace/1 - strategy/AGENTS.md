# Workspace: Strategy

## Purpose
This folder holds durable strategic direction for the business.

## Inheritance
- Inherit all root and workspace-layer rules automatically.

## Rules
- Keep positioning, offer design, market choices, and strategic priorities here.
- Do not store daily execution logs or unrelated working files here.

## Routing
- If the work is about deciding what the business should do, keep it here.
- If the work is about executing a bounded initiative, route it to projects or operations.
