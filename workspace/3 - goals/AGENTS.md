# Workspace: Goals

## Purpose

This folder defines the business goals, planning cycles, measurement logic and campaign mandates for Clínica Kids e Family.

## Inheritance

- Inherit the root and workspace-layer rules automatically.
- Keep this file limited to local routing and durable workspace boundaries.

## Rules

- Organize active planning by annual folder and quarterly cycle: `YYYY/Q#`.
- Keep the active-cycle pointer in the root `INDEX.md` and cycle metadata in the quarter `INDEX.md`.
- Store planning artifacts at the quarter root.
- Keep `campaign-mandates/` and `campaign-feedback/` flat inside the active quarter; dates and periods belong in filenames and frontmatter.
- Use `archived/` only for preserved historical material that is not part of the active cycle.
- Goal Architect owns interpretation and planning; campaign data collection and execution belong to their designated workflows.
- Methodology and schemas come from the invoked Goal Architect skills, not from copied local instructions.

## Routing

- Use this workspace for goals, KPIs, scorecards, planning cycles and campaign mandates.
- Route strategy and brand decisions to `workspace/1 - strategy`.
- Route durable business context to `workspace/2 - context`.
- Route bounded implementation to `workspace/4 - projects`.
