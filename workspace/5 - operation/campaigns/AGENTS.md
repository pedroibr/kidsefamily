# Campaigns

## Purpose

This folder holds operational briefs and assets for bounded marketing campaigns.

## Inheritance

- Inherit all root, workspace, and operation rules automatically.

## Rules

- Keep one folder per campaign and use its `campaign.md` as the central brief and approval record.
- Follow the campaign workflow and approval gates recorded in each campaign's `AGENTS.md` and `campaign.md`.
- Keep campaign-specific decisions in that campaign folder; use shared marketing plans for recurring channel guidance.
- Do not publish, schedule, buy media, or change external platforms from these documents.

## Routing

- Use [Campaign Index](INDEX.md) for the campaign directory map.
- Each campaign folder contains its central brief and assets.
